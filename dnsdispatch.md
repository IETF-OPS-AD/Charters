# DNSDISPATCH

## Overall

DNS-related I-Ds that don't have an obvious WG which could adopt them can be submitted to the DNSOP WG for consideration. The DNSOP WG will advise whether to progress (or not) these I-Ds and will recommend a way to progress a proposal as appropriate. For instance, DNSOP may suggest the most suitable WG or recommend the chartering of a new WG.

## Venue

DNSDISPATCH is traditionally held during plenary DNSOP sessions. However, the process can be run using dedicated virtual interims or via the mailing list. The actual mode used for a given proposal is left to the DNSOP WG Chairs.

## Mode of Operation for Plenary DNSDISPATCH Sessions

- A DNSDISPATCH session may include up to 4 items.
- Chairs will prioritize received requests.
- The Chairs must provide requesters with the reasoning for rejecting a request.
- Proponents of an item have 6 minutes to explain, then followed by 4 minutes of feedback.
- Chairs will summarize the main feedback and gauge consensus.
- If the consensus is "Go to WG X", "Consider a BoF", "Consider AD-Sponsorship", or "Discard because of reason Y", record the outcome. 
   * If no consensus, proponents recommended to continue the discussion on the list.
   * Proponents may request a second DNSDISPATCH slot. In any case, a same item should not be considered for more than two DNSDISPATCH sessions.
- Chairs will summarize and send out an outcomes email to dnsop@ietf.org (for each specific item).

## Coordination

Other IETF areas and IETF-maintained technologies may have their own process for considering new work. While DNSOP is the main DISPATCH home for new DNS-related proposals, some of these proposals may involve other technologies that falls under areas/groups. DNSOP will coordinate and liaise with these areas/groups when processing such proposals.
